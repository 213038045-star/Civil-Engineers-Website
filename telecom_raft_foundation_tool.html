Method & assumptions

  • Bearing capacity from SPT-N uses a settlement-based correlation (Terzaghi/Peck-type): Qa scaled by a depth factor Kd = min(1.33, 1+0.33·Df/L) and by your allowable settlement ÷ 25mm reference basis.
  • Overturning and bearing pressure checks combine both moment directions using a resultant/diagonal (0.707×L) lever-arm approach.
  • Sliding resistance combines a reduced friction term (55% mobilized φ) with an adhesion term from an SPT-correlated cohesion.
  • Pedestal bending credits passive soil pressure resistance (Rankine Kp) against the ground-level shear before sizing the main bars.
  • Column-to-column distance (Wbase) is taken as the side of an equilateral triangle (typical self-supporting 3-legged tower).
This reproduces the original workbook's calculation method as closely as the extracted formulas allow — it is not an independent code derivation. Verify against your own workbook for a real project and have the final design checked by a licensed engineer.
