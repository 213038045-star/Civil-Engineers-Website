Isolated Footing Design

Covers sizing for allowable soil pressure, two‑way (punching) shear, one‑way (beam) shear, flexural design by trial‑and‑error (a / As iteration), minimum reinforcement, column bearing, development length, and optional overturning & uplift checks.

Preliminary design tool for educational / early‑stage use. Formulas follow BNBC 2020 Strength Design provisions (adapted from ACI 318), expressed in US customary units — f'c and fy in psi, forces in kip, plan dimensions in ft, section dimensions (b, d, h, cover, bar dia) in inches. Simplifications: single effective depth "d" used for both reinforcement directions (skin difference between top/bottom layers ignored), square base pressure assumed uniform, flexural steel is sized by direct trial‑and‑error (a / As) iteration rather than the closed‑form quadratic, development length uses the simplified ACI straight‑bar equation assuming (cb+Ktr)/db = 1.5 with ψt=ψe=ψs=λ=1 (coefficient 20 for db ≥ #7, 25 for db < #7). Always verify against project‑specific geotechnical report and have the final design checked/sealed by a licensed structural engineer before construction.
