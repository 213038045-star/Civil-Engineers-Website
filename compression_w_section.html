Compression member (column) design

Sizing a column is iterative — the critical stress Fcr depends on KL/r, which depends on the section you haven't picked yet. Section 02 lets you assume a trial KL/r and back out a target Ag and r. Section 03 checks a real W-shape's actual capacity. Section 04 keeps a log of your trials so you can pick the lightest one that passes — exactly like the manual trial-and-error process.
Pn = Fcr · Ag  |  Fcr = 0.658^(Fy/Fe)·Fy for KL/r ≤ 4.71√(E/Fy), else Fcr = 0.877Fe  |  Fe = π²E/(KL/r)²

02 · Trial iteration — assume KL/r, find required Ag

Since Fcr depends only on KL/r (not on the section itself), assume a slenderness ratio, compute Fcr, then back out the required gross area. Look for a W-shape with r ≥ the required r below and Ag ≥ the required area — then check it for real in section 03. Adjust the assumed KL/r and repeat until it stops improving (this mirrors the manual's Trial-1 / Trial-2 / Trial-3 process).
