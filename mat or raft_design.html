Mat / Raft Foundation Design

Rigid (conventional) method design of a mat/raft foundation to BNBC 2020 (Part 6, Ch. 8 — Strength Design Method, following ACI 318 provisions), in US customary units (kip, ft, in, psi). Covers biaxial eccentric bearing pressure across the raft, punching (two‑way) shear at every column with interior / edge / corner classification, and one‑way shear & flexural strip design in both directions, with an approximate continuous‑beam moment method.

Preliminary design tool for educational / early‑stage use — rigid (conventional) method only (assumes the raft is stiff enough that soil pressure varies linearly across the whole plan area; a flexible/beam‑on‑elastic‑foundation analysis is not modeled here and should be used for very large or lightly‑loaded rafts, or highly non‑uniform column loads/spacing). Key simplifications: net allowable pressure = qa − (γeq/1000)·Df, with raft self‑weight assumed balanced by excavated soil weight (self‑weight not separately added to column loads, matching common isolated‑footing practice); bearing pressure and eccentricity use the biaxial combined‑stress formula q(x,y) = P/A ± Mx·y/Ixx ± My·x/Iyy, taken about the raft's geometric centroid; punching shear at each column uses interior/edge/corner classification (bo and αs per ACI 318 §8.4.4.2) but keeps a simplified full‑rectangle critical area for the tributary subtraction; one‑way shear and flexure use an approximate uniform continuous‑beam coefficient (wln²/16 positive, wln²/10 negative) rather than a full statically‑indeterminate analysis, applied per column‑line strip using tributary width; a single effective depth "d" is used for both reinforcement directions; development length uses the simplified ACI straight‑bar equation assuming (cb+Ktr)/db = 1.5. Always verify against a project‑specific geotechnical report (including settlement, which this tool does not check) and have the final design checked/sealed by a licensed structural engineer before construction.
