⚠ This tool follows the standard classroom worked-example method for a two-flight dog-legged stair with a mid stair-beam: waist-slab + steps dead load, one-way slab moment coefficients (wL²/14 & wL²/9), reinforcement design, and stair-beam flexural design (wL²/14 & wL²/16). It covers flexure only — shear / stirrup design for the stair beam is NOT included and must be checked separately. It assumes a simply idealized two-span-with-landings condition per the lecture method, not a full continuous-beam analysis. Final design must be verified and sealed by a licensed structural engineer.
