Reference basis & assumptions

  • BNBC 2020, Part 6, Ch.6 (Concrete Structures) adopts ACI 318 strength design provisions almost verbatim for shear and flexure; Ch.8 (Foundations) covers pile cap general requirements. This tool applies the ACI 318 sectional (beam-analogy) method — permitted by BNBC/ACI as an alternative to strut-and-tie for pile caps where piles fall outside the strut-and-tie applicability limits.
  • Two-way (punching) shear at the column: φVc = φ·0.33λ√f'c·bo·d, φ = 0.75, checked on a perimeter at d/2 from the column face.
  • One-way shear: φVc = φ·0.17λ√f'c·b·d, φ = 0.75, checked at a section d from the column face.
  • Flexure at column face: As,req = Mu /(φ·fy·0.9d), φ = 0.9; compared against As provided and the code minimum steel ratio.
  • Effective depth is taken as the average of the two reinforcement mats for shear checks, per common two-way footing/pile-cap practice (bottom mat placed as two orthogonal curtains).
  • Pile spacing and edge distance minimums follow common regional practice (CRSI / IS 2911-style guidance): c/c spacing ≥ 3×pile diameter, edge distance ≥ max(150 mm, 0.5×pile diameter).
This tool produces a preliminary sectional check only. It does not cover strut-and-tie verification, punching shear at corner/edge piles, seismic detailing, pile uplift/lateral checks, or serviceability (crack width, deflection). Have the final design verified and stamped by a licensed structural engineer before construction.
