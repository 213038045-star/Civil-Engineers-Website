Multistory Building Pile Design

SPT-based pile capacity (skin friction α/β-method + end bearing, per AASHTO 2007) for a shared soil profile, with an individual pile group design auto-sized (1 to 6 piles) for each building column you enter. Units: kN, m, mm, MPa. Structural checks cover punching (two-way) shear, one-way shear, and flexural design; more specialized checks (column-face shear-friction, hook length, column-base stub design) are out of scope for this tool — see footnote.

Preliminary design tool for educational / early‑stage use. Pile capacity: cumulative layer‑by‑layer skin friction (α‑method for clay: Su=6N, α per the standard reduction beyond Su/101>1.5; β‑method for sand: β=1.5−0.245√z, scaled by N/15 when N≤15) plus end bearing (9·Su for clay; depth/diameter‑dependent Meyerhof‑type correlation for sand), per AASHTO 2007 — derived from the same method used in a tower‑foundation pile design workbook this tool was adapted from. Required pile length per column is solved by linear interpolation of the soil‑capacity‑vs‑depth curve against total group demand (compression = column DL+LL + cap/column‑base self‑weight; uplift = any entered net uplift force − self‑weight), then rounded up to the next 0.5 m with a 1 m safety margin. Pile count auto‑selection: for each column, the tool tries 1 through 6 piles and reports the smallest count where pile length is found, punching shear passes, and one‑way shear passes — it does not check economy beyond pile count (e.g. it won't trade off a larger diameter against fewer piles). Cap geometry for each pile count follows standard pile‑group layouts, with pile‑to‑pile and pile‑to‑edge distances entered as multiples of pile diameter. Punching and one‑way shear use an equivalent smeared pile‑reaction pressure (total factored column axial load ÷ cap plan area) rather than checking each individual pile position — a standard simplification for compact pile groups (default 2.5D spacing) but a coarser approximation for widely‑spaced piles, where individual‑pile punching per ACI 318 §13.2.7 should be checked separately. Flexural design uses a cantilever‑moment formula (Mu = LF·(P·spacing/4 + Mcolumn)) plus an ACI 10.5.3‑style minimum‑reinforcement cascade (including the 4/3·As provision). Not covered by this tool: column‑face shear‑friction checks, hook length past the edge pile, column‑base/stub design (slenderness, main bars, ties), and full multi‑combination load‑case checking (only one combined DL+LL / H / M / Uplift set per column is checked, not separate ACI load combinations) — verify these separately. Always confirm against a project‑specific geotechnical report and have the final design checked/sealed by a licensed structural engineer.
