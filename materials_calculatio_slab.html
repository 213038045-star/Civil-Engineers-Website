Slab Reinforcement Calculator

Inputs: slab dimensions, bar size (#3/#4/#5), and spacing only. Choose cranked bars + extra top, or two straight layers (top & bottom) for each direction.

Slab & Bar Placement (drawn to scale from your inputs)

Each bar type is drawn in its own color (see legend below) — no line-style guessing needed. The orange dashed line marks an interior support/joint (not a bar). Bar counts are sampled for clarity when spacing is dense; spacing and true totals are labeled on the drawing and in the tables below.

Slab Dimensions

Enter each bay's span length separated by commas, e.g. "18, 16.17" for two unequal spans, or "20, 20, 20" for three equal spans. Main (straight/cranked) bars run continuously across all bays, so their length = sum of all spans in that direction. Extra top bars are placed at every support line: at an interior joint, the bar extends into both neighboring spans (span_left÷N + span_right÷N); at the two end supports, it extends into one span only (span÷N).
