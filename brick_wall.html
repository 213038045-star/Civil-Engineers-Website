Foundation
Follows the six-item load path onto wall AB (floor slab, sunshade, lintel, beam reaction, wall self-weight, brickwork GL-to-PL), then the brickwork below GL is added once more to get the load at the top of the footing — used for the compressive stress check. The foundation width is sized from the load at GL plus a first-pass 10%-of-P self-weight estimate, then rechecked against the actual weight of a stepped brick footing (courses widening by the increment above, on PCC + soling layers) until it converges.
