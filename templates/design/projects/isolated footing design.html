Isolated Footing Design

Design of an isolated (square/rectangular) spread footing to BNBC 2020 (Part 6, Ch. 8 — Strength Design Method, following ACI 318 provisions), in US customary units (kip, ft, in, psi). Covers sizing for allowable soil pressure, two‑way (punching) shear, one‑way (beam) shear, flexural design by trial‑and‑error (a / As iteration), minimum reinforcement, column bearing, development length, and optional overturning & uplift checks.

Preliminary design tool for educational / early‑stage use. Formulas follow BNBC 2020 Strength Design provisions (adapted from ACI 318), expressed in US customary units — f'c and fy in psi, forces in kip, plan dimensions in ft, section dimensions (b, d, h, cover, bar dia) in inches. Simplifications: single effective depth "d" used for both reinforcement directions (skin difference between top/bottom layers ignored), square base pressure assumed uniform (moments/shears from the Loads section are used only for the overturning & uplift check, not for eccentric bearing-pressure redistribution), flexural steel is sized by direct trial‑and‑error (a / As) iteration rather than the closed‑form quadratic, development length uses the simplified ACI straight‑bar equation assuming (cb+Ktr)/db = 1.5 with ψt=ψe=ψs=λ=1 (coefficient 20 for db ≥ #7, 25 for db < #7). When "Nominal loads" is used, the governing factored axial (Pu) is taken as the maximum of the seven ASCE 7 basic LRFD combinations (1.4D; 1.2D+1.6L+0.5(Lr+S); 1.2D+1.0L+1.6(Lr+S)+0.5W; 1.2D+1.0L+0.5(Lr+S)+1.0W; 1.2D+1.0L+0.2S+1.0E; 0.9D+1.0W; 0.9D+1.0E), service axial (Pa) is the sum of the gravity cases D+L+Lr+S, and uplift demand is taken from the more severe of the two 0.9D combinations. Always verify against project‑specific geotechnical report and have the final design checked/sealed by a licensed structural engineer before construction.
