UNITS: ft / in / ksi / psf
REF: ACI 318 Ch.8 & 318-63 M3 (BNBC Part 6, Ch.6)
⚠ Preliminary strength design (USD) using the ACI/BNBC approximate moment-coefficient method for continuous one-way slabs — extended to four spans, with every support (both exterior ends and all interior supports) resting on beams rather than walls. Coefficients shown are the standard ACI Table 12.1 values for three-or-more-span layouts. Verify applicability of each coefficient for your actual support/continuity conditions, and have final design checked by a licensed structural engineer.

Moment coefficients (ACI Table 12.1, three-or-more spans): end-span positive = wul2/11 (discontinuous end unrestrained); interior-span positive = wul2/16; negative at first interior support (each end) = wul2/10; negative at other interior supports = wul2/11; exterior (end) support negative = 0 by default (discontinuous, unrestrained — increase if the end beam actually restrains rotation); cantilever = wul2/2
Flexural design (trial-and-error, successive approximation): a=Asfy/(0.85f'cb), As=Mu/[φfy(d−a/2)] — iterate until a converges, φ=0.90
Tension-controlled limit (over-reinforcement check): ρt=0.85β1(f'c/fy)×0.375 at εt=0.005; β1=0.85 for f'c≤4 ksi, reduced 0.05 per 1 ksi above 4 (floor 0.65). If ρ=As/(bd) > ρt, section is over-reinforced (not ductile) — increase t, raise f'c, or reduce Mu
