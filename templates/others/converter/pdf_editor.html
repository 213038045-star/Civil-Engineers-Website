Open a PDF. Select/Edit Text edits existing text. Add Text creates a new editable text box. Move Text lets you drag text, cells/grids, rectangles, images or signatures. Resize an Excel Grid by selecting it and dragging its blue handles. Erase Cell Side removes one outside border side of the selected grid. Draw Line lets you click-drag a straight line. Whiteout lets you click-drag a rectangle to cover an area. Crop lets you crop a selected picture or signature. Erase Text lets you click existing text to remove it instantly.
