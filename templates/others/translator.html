Common joint letters (st, nt, nd, bd, kt, pr, tr...) form automatically; other letters stay separate (korbe = করবে). To force a joint letter put _ between them (k_j = ক্জ). Capital T D = ট ড.

Tip: drag a box on the picture around just the part you want to read (for example only the card). This makes the reading much more accurate. To skip a part (like a logo), press Ignore area (or hold Shift) and drag over it: it turns green.
