Reference basis & formulas

  • Optimum asphalt content (OAC) is taken as the asphalt content at the target design air voids (default 4%, the median of the typical 3–5% design range) on the Va-vs-AC curve, found by linear interpolation between the two bracketing trial specimens. All other properties at OAC (stability, flow, VMA, VFA, density) are interpolated the same way.
  • Voids in mineral aggregate: VMA = 100 − (Gmb·Ps)/Gsb, where Ps is aggregate percent by total mix weight (100 − Pb).
  • Air voids: Va = 100·(Gmm − Gmb)/Gmm.
  • Voids filled with asphalt: VFA = 100·(VMA − Va)/VMA.
  • Absorbed asphalt: Pba = 100·Gb·(Gse − Gsb)/(Gsb·Gse). Effective asphalt content: Pbe = Pb − Pba·Ps/100.
  • Marshall criteria (compaction blows and stability/flow/VFA limits) follow the traffic-level bands commonly published in AASHTO R-12 / Asphalt Institute MS-2: light traffic uses 35 blows/side, medium 50, heavy 75; the minimum VMA depends on nominal maximum aggregate size (NMAS). Treat these as standard starting points — always confirm against your specifying agency's current standard.
  • The phase diagram splits total mix volume into air (Va), effective asphalt binder (VMA − Va), and aggregate (100 − VMA) at the interpolated OAC.
This tool implements the classic Marshall mix design method (AASHTO R-12 / Asphalt Institute MS-2) for preliminary/educational mix proportioning. It does not cover Superpave gyratory design, moisture-susceptibility (TSR) testing, or performance-grade binder selection. Have the final mix design verified by a qualified materials engineer and laboratory testing before production.
